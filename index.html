Who's building?

I'm a builder from Kerala, India, who ends up taking things apart to see what's underneath — sometimes literally. My projects sit at the point where an idea stops being software and starts needing a soldering iron.

I work across both worlds: HTML and CSS on the front end, Python and JavaScript underneath, driving whatever hardware the project actually needs. Software gets an idea running. Hardware is what makes it real — and that's the part I keep coming back to.

Most of what I make starts as a question I can't stop thinking about, not a spec someone handed me.

Brain–computer interface

Neuro‑controlled wheelchair

My major build so far: reading live signals straight from the neurons and using them to drive a wheelchair in real time, with no perceptible latency between intent and motion. It's the project that pulled neuroscience and robotics onto the same bench for me — signal acquisition, filtering, and control logic all having to work together fast enough to feel instant.
